# Sponsorship

MaintainerWatch is an independent, open-source research preview. Sponsorship
supports maintenance, validation data work and the small amount of
infrastructure needed to keep the public dataset and CI index current.

**GitHub Sponsors:** <https://github.com/sponsors/ovidiurg>

The GitHub Sponsors profile is live. The Sponsor button is configured through
`.github/FUNDING.yml`.

## Suggested tiers

These are funding levels, not a promise of security outcomes:

| Tier | Monthly | One-time | What it supports |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Supporter | $5 | $25 | Project updates; no public attribution required |
| Backer | $25 | $100 | Optional name/logo recognition; quarterly roadmap note |
| Organization | $100 | $250 | Optional organization recognition; quarterly maintenance briefing |

Sponsors can choose any amount in the GitHub Sponsors flow. Recurring support
is more useful than one-time support because validation and data refreshes are
ongoing costs.

## What sponsorship pays for

- registry and advisory ingestion, scoring and validation refreshes;
- validation datasets, label intake and calibration review;
- the compact CI index, release checks and basic hosting;
- documentation, issue triage and maintenance.

## What sponsorship does not buy

- influence over scores, findings, alerts or disclosures;
- early access to vulnerability or incident information;
- removal of findings, allow-listing or reputation changes;
- ranked target lists, maintainer dossiers or offensive reconnaissance;
- a security guarantee or a promise that a package is safe.

A high score is a review request, not proof of compromise. Findings and
disclosures follow the project's security and dual-use rules.

## Transparency and privacy

- Public sponsor names or logos are shown only with consent.
- Sponsorship does not require a public name.
- Payments and tax handling are provided through GitHub Sponsors and its
  payment processor. Sponsorship may be taxable income; maintainers are
  responsible for their own tax and accounting.
- Support does not create a customer, employment or security-services
  relationship.
